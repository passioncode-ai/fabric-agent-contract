## Decisions

- No new job state or member: the job schema is closed and its states are MCP Task states, so the position travels in
  `statusMessage` and a full queue is a refusal at start; no `fabric-interop` revision.
- `listed` only, no `visible`: an entry a viewer may not read is absent.
- Partner scopes (DEC-0090) are not a directory source here; left for a later revision.
