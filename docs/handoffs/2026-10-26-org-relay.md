Decide the organization relay's handshake (CT-37), the last open piece of the organization layer on the contract's
side: what a member signs in `auth.estate`, what `welcome` carries, when an open session stands.

## Done

- DEC-0134: [relay](../specification/relay.md#the-organizations-relay), OR-1 to OR-5.
  - **auth** names the organization's context id in `estate`, never a member's estate id; `admit` is unchanged.

  - **Sessions** stand under the list's admission set (`sessionStands` now takes it, `SessionAdmission`).
  - **No organization heartbeat or delta is needed:** the list's 7-day window and OL-5's draft on every end bound how
    long an ended member stays admitted.
