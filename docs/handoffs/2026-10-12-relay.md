# Handoff — the relay and the secret envelope (DEC-0047; `fabric-relay/0.1`, `fabric-secret-envelope/1`)

## Objective

Decide the relay protocol and the secret envelope for the relay server
([passioncode-ai/relay](https://github.com/passioncode-ai/relay), its RLY-02), Project Observatory's node and Fabric's
surfaces, after the operator's D6 (2026-10-12): the relay is its own open repository, self-hosting stays (V2-21).

## Done

- **DEC-0047** in [`docs/DECISIONS.md`](../DECISIONS.md), with [relay](../specification/relay.md) as its text.
  Rule codes **FAC-SEM-070** (`secret-envelope`) and **FAC-SEM-071** (`relay-frame`), receipts SEM-0070/0071.
- Schemas: `schemas/relay-frame.schema.json`, `schemas/secret-envelope.schema.json`.
- Code: `src/relay.ts` (admission, `relay-auth/1`, `relay-blob/1`, the message body), `src/secret-envelope.ts` (HPKE
  base mode checked against RFC 9180 §A.3.1, seal and open), `src/relay-rules.ts`. `src/p256.ts` merged with
  DEC-0053's copy, and given one helper that turns SEC 1 octets or a JWK into the key every check uses.
- Vectors: `fixtures/vectors/relay.json` (an Ed25519 participant refused as `unsupported-alg`, the machine key in
  both spellings), `fixtures/vectors/secret-envelope.json`, `fixtures/vectors/p256.json`. They were made with one-off
  keys whose private halves were discarded; the envelope's recipient is RFC 9180's `pkRm`.
- The contract owner's review of #53 is answered in full: 11 blocking items, 10 non-blocking, and the rulings:
  - T1 is DL-25's same owner;
  - presence stays within one owner;
  - ES256 with a signed `alg`;
  - one `{sequence, sha256}` head;
  - not-before;
  - a signed `exp`.
- Neighbours repointed: `devices.md` and the check-in schema's `records` descriptions, which now point to DEC-0054
  (reserved) and the window lease's decision. DEC-0053's own text is unchanged, being append-only.
- Sources: RFC 9180, RFC 6455 and RFC 8446 rows. Coverage floors: `relay.ts`, `secret-envelope.ts`, `relay-rules.ts`
  and `p256.ts` at 100 %. `CONTEXT.md`: Relay, Admission set, Secret envelope.
