## Decisions taken here

- **The adapter's codes are kept for the token's fault** (`unauthenticated` / `approval-invalid`, `kid-not-certified`)
  and for an unreadable source (`issuer-unavailable`, `estate-unreadable`).
- **A revision outside its window is the trust source's fault** (`issuer-unavailable`, `estate-outside-window`), where
  the adapter answers `kid-not-certified`. DEC-0045's wire gives `issuer-unavailable` to unusable published keys, and
  minting again does not cure an expired revision.
- **Only an absent `estate` skips the check.** `null` or a non-object is unreadable.
- **A `kid` on two machines certifies neither.**
- **The rollback and the fork are refused** when the verifier passes its applied mark (`estate-rolled-back`,
  `estate-forked`).
- **An unreadable clock is refused before row 1** in all three verifiers. This is an adjacent defect fixed here: a NaN
  clock let an expired token through.
- **`hubKeyCertified` lives in its own module**, so a callee that vendors `src/delegation.ts` needs no WebAuthn or
  package code.
