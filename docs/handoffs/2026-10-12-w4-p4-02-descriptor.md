## Decisions taken here

- An offer is `peer` beside `placement`, not a third placement value: a node-hosted listing is a local placement under
  the node, a cloud listing a remote one, and their rules stay.
- The data classes are the organization edition's four (device policy's, DEC-0053), compared exactly as written; the
  contract's own `dataClass` vocabulary is refused there, not mapped.
- `residency`: the shape only — two upper-case letters, the user-assigned codes refused, `EU` admitted; no assignment
  check and no residency matching.
- `runner.credentialKind`: `subscription`, `key`, `platform-metered` (Fabric Inbox's B-87 item 5), `none`; `kind` is
  required only for a subscription. A peer must carry `runner` so the node can check it.
- `installedBy` keeps its string form; the object form names `person` (with `operator` or `administrator`), `node` or
  `organization`, and the installer as `tool`.
- `label`/`plist` are refused beside `node`, like `unit`/`task`; the node keeps its children in its own record.
- The node restarts a child no sooner than 10 s after its last start, as the other supervisors do.
- The usage report for listings is the same object: `platform-metered` added, nothing else; per-member sums stay out.
- The "Next free" rule-code marker is set to `FAC-SEM-089` in the patch so G-13 passes with it applied; the owner
  session sets it at merge. Without the patch, G-13 fails on this branch (the four codes have no register rows).
