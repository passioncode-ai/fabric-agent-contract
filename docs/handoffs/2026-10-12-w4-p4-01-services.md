## Decisions taken here

- Markers that disagree exit 75 at once **whether or not the lock is held**, before the lock is tried: the ruling says
  "exits 75 at once naming the conflict", and a supervisor file that says two things is a defect to show, not to run.
- An empty `FABRIC_SERVICE_SUPERVISOR` is no marker; `--fabric-supervisor=<m>` is accepted beside the two-element form
  (argument parsers accept both); the argument is read after the executable and before `--`.
- The marker admits `node` now (the agreed supervisor list); the descriptor's `lifecycle.manager: node` is P4-02's.
- `label`/`plist` stay valid beside `none` (F-B-209: refusing them there is not additive-safe).
- The reader refuses an ACE type other than allow and deny, and ignores an allow ACE with a zero mask.
- The "Next free" rule-code marker in `conformance.md` is set to `FAC-SEM-080`, against the wave's instruction to leave
  it: G-13 fails the gate while an allocated code is at or above the marker. The owner session sets it at merge.
